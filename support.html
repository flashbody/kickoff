FAQ

How do I restore my purchase?

Open Kickoff → Settings → "Restore Purchases". Sign in with the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.

Why is a match not updating?

Kickoff uses live data from API-Football. Occasionally there may be a delay. Pull down to refresh the match list.

Can I use Kickoff on iPad?

Yes. Kickoff supports iPad with Split View and multitasking.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Go to Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → Kickoff to cancel or modify.
